  ### Key design decisions worth relaying
  1. `Unknown` outcome is treated as a legitimate E2B value (weak, watched
  share), not a placeholder; `UNKNOWN` country is still an error via the ISO
  regex.
  2. Uniqueness is on the composite grain (case_id, product, event_term);
  `case_id` alone is not unique in a case-event listing. The rules file says how
  to switch if the vendor confirms one row per case.
  3. Across-month monitoring uses XmR (individuals) charts with limits frozen on
  the first 6 months, because binomial limits at n=5,000 are far too tight for
  a vendor process; the generic profiler's within-file p-chart and date-spike
  heuristic are not informative for a single-month file.
  4. Accuracy is only measurable via the human FAM pass and a 20-record/month
  trace-back to the safety database; the plan says so plainly and schedules
  both.
  5. Right-data concerns flagged for the signal-detection lead: whether the
  disproportionality background is the company portfolio only, and that eight
  fields lack case version, suspect/concomitant role, onset date, age and sex.

  What the research found

  No joint paper by all three exists, and none of them has written on data
  quality in Significance. Their intersection is the RSS Real World Data Science
  platform: Redman and Hoerl are columnists, Tate runs the "Data Quality Under
  the Lens" column, and all three converge on the 22 October 2026 Edinburgh
  conference your repo is named for. The verified corpus is 60-odd items, listed
  with URLs in bibliography.md
  (.claude/skills/data-quality-evaluation/references/bibliography.md). The skill
  draws on Redman's Friday Afternoon Measurement, perfect-record score, rule of
  ten and fix-at-source modes; Hoerl's data pedigree, "right data" versus "data
  are right", statistical thinking and SPC on data; and Tate's tiered
  definitions, triangulation, unit-by-period metrics, five failure patterns and
  initial data analysis.

  The skill lives at .claude/skills/data-quality-evaluation/:

  - SKILL.md (.claude/skills/data-quality-evaluation/SKILL.md) gives a
    seven-step workflow from framing the data customer to a verdict, plus a
    plan-design branch and a report template.
